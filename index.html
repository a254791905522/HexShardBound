The art of the bound

  1. Leap to a neighbor — Tap any adjacent hex to bound forward; fractured tiles cannot be crossed again.
  2. Clear the quota — Destroy enough of the grid to trigger the escape sequence at the exit tile.
  3. Read the terrain — Thick tiles survive an extra jump; ice slides you until you hit a wall or fall off.
  4. Spend your coins — Undo, extra moves, bonus time, or skip a nightmare level when you are cornered.
