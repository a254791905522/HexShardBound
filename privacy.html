Privacy for players

Hex Shard Bound

How this hex-grid puzzle app may collect, use, and share information when you play on iOS—written for clarity, not as a contract.

Applies to

Hex Shard Bound on iPhone and iPad (App Store build). This page does not describe unrelated sites or services.

Ads in the app

Recent versions may display advertisements. When an ad loads, third-party ad partners process data under their own policies.

What Apple discloses

May be linked to you

Usage data

The App Store privacy label for Hex Shard Bound indicates that usage-related information may be collected and associated with your identity for app functionality, analytics, or related purposes. Exact categories can change when the app is updated—check the live “App Privacy” section on the store listing.

May be used to track you

Usage data across apps and sites

The same label states that usage data may be used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, for example when advertising or measurement partners are involved. Your iOS privacy settings influence what is permitted on your device.

On your device

Gameplay and progress

Level state, coins, settings, and similar puzzle progress are typically stored in the app sandbox on your device so you can continue without a separate in-game account.

No account required

Core play does not depend on you registering a profile with the game. Data tied to Apple ID for download, updates, or purchases is handled by Apple.

Your controls

  • Delete local game data Remove the app or clear its storage in iOS Settings to wipe sandbox data on that device.
  • Limit tracking and personalized ads Use Settings → Privacy & Security, including App Tracking Transparency where available, plus Screen Time and content restrictions. Options vary by iOS version and region.
  • Review the store label after updates When a new version ships, open the App Store listing and read the current “App Privacy” details before assuming practices are unchanged.

This summary is informational only and is not legal advice. If it differs from the installed app, the App Store “App Privacy” label, or partner policies shown when an ad appears, those sources govern. Practices may change when features or SDKs are updated. No personal developer contact details or off-product websites are listed here.
